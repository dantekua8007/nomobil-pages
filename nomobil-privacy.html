Nomobil 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年9月

Nomobil（以下称"本 App"）是一款帮助个人经营者记录和整理消费单据的工具。本政策说明我们如何处理你的数据。

1. 我们收集什么

数据类型说明
单据照片你拍摄或上传的收据、发票图片
财务信息从单据中识别或你手动填写的商家名称、金额、日期、类别、支付方式等
你自行配置的 API Key用于连接第三方 AI 识别服务，存储在你设备的系统级安全存储（Keychain）中

2. 数据存储在哪里

本 App 不运营任何服务器，不会把你的单据数据上传到 Nomobil 或其开发者控制的任何服务器。所有记录和原图都只保存在你的 iPhone 本地存储中。

3. 数据会发送给谁

Nomobil 采用"自带密钥"（BYOK）模式：AI 识别功能需要你自己在设置页填入第三方 AI 服务商的 API Key（如 Anthropic Claude、Google Gemini，或你自行配置的其他兼容服务）。当你使用拍照识别功能时：

  • 单据照片和识别请求会由你的设备直接发送给你自己选择、自己付费的第三方 AI 服务商，用于识别照片内容；
  • Nomobil 的开发者不会经手、查看或存储这次传输中的任何内容；
  • 该第三方服务商如何处理这些数据，受其自身的隐私政策约束，请在使用前自行查阅你所选服务商的隐私政策。

4. 你的 API Key 如何保存

你填入的 API Key 存储在 iOS 系统级的 Keychain 中，不会被明文写入普通应用存储，也不会被上传到任何服务器。删除 App 或在设置中清除后，本机上的 Key 会一并移除。

5. 我们不做的事

  • 不建立用户账户系统，不要求注册
  • 不做云同步或跨设备备份
  • 不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的行为
  • 不向广告商或数据经纪商出售或分享任何数据

6. 数据删除

删除记录后，数据先进入回收站，7 天后连同原图一起从设备上永久物理删除，或你可以随时手动清空回收站。卸载 App 会移除本机上的全部数据。

7. 儿童隐私

本 App 面向企业经营者进行日常记账管理，不面向儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 政策更新

如本政策有重大变更，我们会更新本页面顶部的"最后更新"日期。

9. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过以下方式联系我们：
邮箱：nomobil.my@gmail.com

Nomobil Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nomobil ("the App") helps small business owners and freelancers capture and organize expense receipts. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. What We Collect

Data TypeDescription
Receipt photosPhotos of receipts and invoices you capture or upload
Financial informationMerchant name, amount, date, category, payment method — either extracted from receipts or entered manually
Your own API keyUsed to connect to a third-party AI recognition service, stored in your device's system-level secure storage (Keychain)

2. Where Data Is Stored

Nomobil does not operate any server. Your receipt data is never uploaded to Nomobil or to any server controlled by its developer. All records and original images are stored only on your iPhone's local storage.

3. Who Data Is Shared With

Nomobil uses a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) model: the AI recognition feature requires you to enter your own API key for a third-party AI provider (such as Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or another compatible service you configure). When you use the photo recognition feature:

  • The receipt photo and recognition request are sent directly from your device to the third-party AI provider you chose and pay for, in order to recognize the content;
  • The Nomobil developer never receives, views, or stores any content from that transmission;
  • How that third-party provider handles this data is governed by its own privacy policy — please review the privacy policy of whichever provider you choose before use.

4. How Your API Key Is Stored

The API key you enter is stored in the iOS system-level Keychain. It is not written in plain text to ordinary app storage, and it is never uploaded to any server. Deleting the App or clearing it in Settings removes the key from your device.

5. What We Don't Do

  • No user account system, no registration required
  • No cloud sync or cross-device backup
  • No tracking of your activity across other apps or websites
  • We never sell or share any data with advertisers or data brokers

6. Data Deletion

Deleted records first go to the Recycle Bin, then are permanently and physically deleted from the device — along with the original images — after 7 days, or you may empty the Recycle Bin manually at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on the device.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is designed for business owners managing day-to-day bookkeeping. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Updates

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
