常见问题

Q：我需要注册账号吗？
不需要。Nomobil 不要求账户注册，所有数据保存在你的设备本地。

Q：识别功能需要什么条件？
你需要在设置页填入自己的 AI 服务商 API Key（如 Anthropic Claude 或 Google Gemini），Key 由你自行申请和付费，App 本身免费。

Q：删除的记录还能找回吗？
可以，删除后的记录会在"回收站"里保留 7 天，超过 7 天后自动永久清除。

Q：数据会同步到云端吗？
不会。目前所有数据只存储在本机，卸载 App 会清除本机数据。

更多信息请见 隐私政策。

FAQ

Q: Do I need to create an account?
No. Nomobil doesn't require account registration — all data is stored locally on your device.

Q: What do I need for the recognition feature to work?
You need to enter your own API key from an AI provider (such as Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini) in Settings. You obtain and pay for the key yourself; the App itself is free.

Q: Can I recover deleted records?
Yes. Deleted records stay in the Recycle Bin for 7 days before being permanently removed.

Q: Is my data synced to the cloud?
No. All data currently lives only on your device. Uninstalling the App removes local data.

See the Privacy Policy for more information.
